Toque

Household chores logged with a tap of the phone on an NFC tag. Tap the tag on the washing machine and Toque starts counting; when the cycle ends it lets you know, and if the laundry is still inside later, it lets you know again. Chores do not need another list. They need something that remembers at the right moment, and with a tag where the chore happens, marking it takes a second.

Details

  • No login at the tag: each tag holds a link with its own token, which opens that chore and nothing else.
  • Notifications through ntfy, with Done and Snooze buttons and quiet hours.
  • An optional Google Calendar of its own, with each event removed once the chore is done.
  • Installable as an app on the iPhone home screen.
  • Fits in free tiers: Next.js on Vercel, Postgres on Neon and a cron job every minute.

Try it
